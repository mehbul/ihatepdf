# Keyword research and page map

Prepared 26 October 2026 for PDFGirlfriend.

## Delivered

- keyword-page-map.csv: 57 candidate searches grouped into 19 real task pages. Each primary/related query maps to one preferred URL rather than a duplicate synonym page. Fidelity guardrails prevent copy from promising unsupported outputs.
- keyword-planner-seeds.txt: unique seed searches ready for Keyword Planner research. These are product-derived candidates, not proven search demand.

## Research status

The signed-in Google Ads account selector reported no Ads accounts. No account was created, billing entered, campaign started or money spent. Keyword Planner searches/volume estimates are therefore not available. All volume, geography and reporting-period cells are deliberately blank; there is no claim that 100,000 keywords were tested. The owner said to build the map while access is unavailable.

Existing Google visible-result evidence from 26 October shows major established PDF brands for free pdf tools and merge pdf online; the owner's site was absent from those first pages. That is a regional snapshot, not a difficulty score or demand estimate. Current Bing homepage indexing is confirmed separately; it does not establish ranking.

## Architecture decisions

Keep the existing static template and registry architecture. The /pdf-converter/ hub links to supported conversion tasks; /tools/ describes actual capability and /about/ explains identity and privacy. Existing guides demonstrate merge, extraction and compression. Existing articles answer comparison and privacy intent. All 42 public pages were reachable from the homepage within three links in the same-day link audit.

Prefer merge, extraction, organization, image conversion and clearly distinguished compression outputs because they are direct supported tasks, not because demand or conversion rates have been measured. Office conversion and OCR pages must lead with their fidelity/browser limits. Experimental redaction must not target a promise of permanent sensitive-data removal. No pages are proposed for unavailable verified sanitization or validated PDF/A.

No Webflow migration, repetitive synonym pages, city pages or expanded competitor pages are justified by this candidate list alone. Existing metadata/schema and explicit limits already cover these task intents. No copy was rewritten merely to repeat seed phrases.

## Next data-backed step

Use an existing owner-authorized Keyword Planner account or owner-provided CSV. Record country, language, period, source and returned ranges exactly; ad competition and CPC are advertising metrics, not organic ranking difficulty. Evaluate English India and worldwide separately, never add overlapping regional totals. Group new related terms by user outcome, map them to existing pages, and propose a new page only for a distinct supported task with useful original content. Use actual Search Console queries/clicks and output feedback to prioritize changes. No rank, AI citation, visitor count or revenue forecast is inferred from estimated search volume.
